# Brand Assets

The following files are official TRMNL glyphs published for download on TRMNL's [Brand Assets](https://trmnl.com/brand) page:

- `nodes/Trmnl/trmnl.svg` — black glyph for light backgrounds, downloaded from `https://trmnl.com/images/brand/glyphs/glyph--black.svg`
- `nodes/Trmnl/trmnl.dark.svg` — white glyph for dark backgrounds, downloaded from `https://trmnl.com/images/brand/glyphs/glyph--white.svg`

The glyph artwork is used unmodified. TRMNL owns its brand assets and trademarks. The repository's MIT license does not grant additional rights to TRMNL's artwork or marks.
